# A collaboration with Sacha Jafri

During Anton's 2021 exhibition at Leila Heller Gallery in Dubai, the British artist Sacha Jafri was showing
at the same gallery. Across two very different practices, the two struck up a collaboration. Jafri, known
for his large, gestural abstract paintings, shared digital textures of his paintings with Anton, who
wrapped them onto the surfaces of his own lattice-path sculptures in a set of digital renders.

The result joins two opposite instincts: Anton's precise, symmetrical geometry and Jafri's free, splattered
colour. In the renders the sculptures appear in a vivid red, their inner faces opening onto Jafri's
abstraction, so each form holds both order and improvisation at once.
