# Abu Dhabi Art (2021)

Anton's work has also reached major international art fairs. In November 2021, around the time of his
Dubai gallery debut, his sculptures were presented at Abu Dhabi Art, one of the region's leading
international fairs, on the stand of Leila Heller Gallery. Among the pieces on view were gold and
mirror-polished works, shown alongside other artists the gallery represents.

The fair appearance is part of a wider pattern: Anton's geometric, symmetrical work has found a natural
audience in the Middle East, where it sits comfortably within the long local tradition of geometric form.
