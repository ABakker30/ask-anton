# Alternative Perspective (MoMath, 2021)

In 2021 the National Museum of Mathematics (MoMath) in New York presented Alternative Perspective, a
show of Anton Bakker's work in its gallery, Composite. Because of the pandemic it opened first as a
virtual gallery, with online "meet the artist" receptions (a members' preview and a public opening), and
later ran as an in-person exhibit on the museum floor.

The show's premise is the heart of Anton's work: a change in perspective seems to change the very reality
of the object in front of you. His sculptures fix points in space that, as the eye connects them, resolve
into three-dimensional paths. Across the exhibit those paths take many forms: lines, curves, knots,
spirals, Mobius strips, optical illusions, and fractals, executed in steel, bronze, and as digital
interactives. Several pieces sit on slowly rotating bases under directed light, so each casts shifting
shadows on the walls around it, a small staging of the lesson Awakening makes: what you see depends on
where you stand.

The works were grouped into themed collections, among them Stars and Optical Illusions, each paired with
an interactive digital experience. One set of wall images presents selected perspectives of Opus 548001,
"Ode to M.C. Escher."

Anton's pieces were complemented by two guest contributions: a surprising work by engineers-turned-artists
Walt van Ballegooijen and Hans Kuiper, and a mathematical sculpture by former Bell Labs scientist Alan
White.
