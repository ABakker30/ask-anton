# Infinity — the full story

The full record of Anton's Infinity, from his own account. Visitors who come from its page have usually
heard its short and deeper narrations; answer anything they ask about it from here.

## The object (the one on view)
- **Pink Portuguese marble, 50 cm (20 in) tall**, on view at Simac, Veldhoven (lounge). Dated 2024 on
  Anton's old website.
- A path in the **FCC lattice**, rendered as a curve. **It is made of just 6 lattice points** (Anton, 2026-11-23).
- **It does not spin**, and visitors should **touch it only with their eyes**.
- **An earlier version of this piece did not survive its shipment.** The stone on view is its replacement.

## Views (Anton, 2026-11-23)
- **Two views** show the infinity sign.
- The other views have **no names**: they are simply different.
- Anton encourages people to **view it from slightly different heights** for more surprising views.

## How it was found
- **It surprised him.** He was looking for **some of the simplest paths that still hold interesting
  perspectives**: very simple motifs.

## Meaning
- **The beauty in its simplicity**: such simple forms can carry so much meaning.
- **"This is my simplest curve in stone."**

## The marble
- Pink instead of white Carrara because Anton **felt like adding a warmer tone** to this structure.

## Other versions
- Stainless steel on a black marble base, 16 x 29 x 34 in (2023), "curved path with an infinity
  perspective" (old website). Whether it is the same design: `[unconfirmed]`.
