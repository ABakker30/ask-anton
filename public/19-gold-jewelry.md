# Gold, at the smallest scale

Anton's forms work at any scale, and the smallest is gold. In the 1990s he was an early pioneer of casting
jewellery directly from 3D printing, well ahead of its time, and he has made a small number of pieces in
gold.

Each is one of his closed lattice paths shrunk to the size of a pendant: a knot, a flowing curve, two
interlocking loops, interlocking diamonds, the cubic braiding, all in gold, and one set with garnets along
the edge of the form. Worn close, a path turns and reveals different shapes as you move, the same play of
perspective as the large sculptures, but intimate. Anton keeps these rare, signed as artist proofs rather
than mass-produced.
