# Anton's geometry at architectural scale

Anton has always loved architecture, and in particular the way these ancient geometric shapes can live at
a large scale. Alongside his sculptures he renders his lattice-path forms at the scale of buildings and
city monuments.

In one strand, the forms become buildings: a zigzag or angular path drawn up into a tower, its glass
facade carrying the same kind of interlocking geometric pattern that runs through Islamic ornament and
through the lattice itself, with planted terraces folded into the structure. In another, the sculptures
stand as monumental public works on a city plaza or skyline, the fractal tree among them, scaled so that
people and cars pass beneath them, and imagined within a Dubai cityscape.

These are renderings, studies of an ambition Anton has carried for years: to bring his geometry into the
built environment, especially in the Middle East, where the long geometric tradition makes such forms feel
at home.
