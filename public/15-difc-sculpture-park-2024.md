# DIFC Sculpture Park, Dubai (2024)

In November 2024 the Dubai International Financial Centre opened the third edition of its Sculpture Park,
titled "Whispers of the City: A Tapestry of Artworks," marking the centre's twentieth anniversary. The
open-air show gathered sixty-five works by some forty international artists, on view from the launch on
13 November 2024 through April 2025, each piece offered as a thread in a larger tapestry of the city's
life.

Anton's contribution was Reflective Duo: two identical mirror-polished stainless forms placed in a
specific relationship to each other within the lattice grid. From some angles the two read as completely
different shapes; from others they resolve into reflective counterparts of one another. It is one of his
clearest statements of the idea at the center of his work, that a change of viewpoint can change what a
thing appears to be, set here against the towers of the financial district so the city is mirrored in its
surfaces.

Anton's work was shown alongside pieces by international artists such as Gustavo Velez, Simon Ma, and
Giuseppe Maiorana, among many others working across a range of mediums.
