# Koos Knoopje — the full story

The full record of Koos Knoopje (Dutch for "Koos's little knot", Opus 325846), from Anton's own
account. Visitors who come from its page have usually heard its short and deeper narrations; answer
anything they ask about it from here.

## Origin (Anton, 2026-11-23)
- **Koos created the knot in a different lattice: BCC.** His version was polylinear (straight segments).
- **Anton discovered the knot in FCC, and curved it.**
- **Koos became a big fan of the curved shapes.** Anton named this, the **initial curved knot**, in Koos's
  name, based on Koos's version in a different lattice.

## Knots (Anton, 2026-11-23)
- Both Koos and Anton have a **fascination for knotted structures**: they **cannot be fully untangled into
  a plain loop without cutting the path**.
- Anton wonders about knots **not just in sculpture**: **how do knots form in situations?**
- The form is a figure-eight knot.

## The form and its views
- From Anton's Norfolk statement: "Four petals repeat, each bisected by perfect symmetry." "From one
  angle it stands upright, from another it inverts."
- Walk a quarter of the way around and the knot appears to turn upside down.

## The boy at the art fair (confirmed 2026-11-23)
- The knot in the story **was this same knot form, 8 ft, stainless steel** (LA art fair). Anton asked a
  boy of about 5 to 7 to help turn it upside down; "too heavy"; look from another angle: by its symmetry
  it already is. Then: "now ask your father to help you turn it upside down."

## Versions
- Stainless steel, 8 ft (the art-fair one).
- Painted steel, red, 8 ft: Norfolk Botanical Gardens.
- Patinated bronze, 5 ft (2021): Norfolk Botanical Gardens.
- Stainless steel, 5 ft (2021).
- **Carrara marble, 50 cm: on view at Simac, Veldhoven** (meeting room).
