# The Koos Puzzle App (koospuzzle.com)

## What it is
**koospuzzle.com** is the free web app where Anton's ball puzzle lives today. It runs in any
browser, installs on a phone like a native app, needs no account to play, and speaks twelve
languages. Everything in it grows from one object: the 25 four-sphere pieces in the FCC
lattice.

## How playing works
You solve by **drawing pieces directly on the board**: tap four connected spheres, one at a
time, and if they form one of the 25 legal pieces it snaps into place. There is no tray of
pieces to drag — pieces emerge from the spheres you choose. A built-in **tutorial** ("Show
me how", about sixty seconds) teaches this on flat single-layer boards before moving to a
first 3D shape, and a demo will even place a piece by itself so you can watch the gesture
before trying it.

If you get stuck, a **hint** places a correct piece for you — but hinted pieces don't count
as yours. The app can also **check solvability**: whether your current position can still
be completed at all.

## Scoring: placements, then time
A result is written as **X/N** — how many of the N pieces you placed yourself (hints cost
you exactly the piece they place, nothing more). Between two solvers, more self-placements
wins; equal placements break to the faster time. A **perfect solve is N/N with no hints**.

## Difficulty categories
Puzzles are classified by facts of their shape: **2D** (single-layer, where the tutorial
lives), **Easy** (up to 16 spheres), **Medium** (to 40), **Hard** (to 96), and **Nearly
impossible** (100 spheres and beyond — the classic full-set shapes, with search spaces past
10⁹⁰). The gallery can be filtered by these.

## Ways to play against others
- **Challenge links (ghost races).** After a solve you can share a short link
  (koospuzzle.com/c/…). Whoever opens it races your **recorded solve replayed live** —
  your pieces appear on their board at the exact moments you placed them. Beat the ghost
  and the dare becomes yours to send onward.
- **The Challenges tab** in the gallery lists open dares anyone has posted — pick one and
  race without waiting for a link.
- **Leaderboards** exist per puzzle, one best entry per solver; tapping any entry races
  that person's ghost.
- **Live matches.** You can invite a specific person by code or link for a turn-based
  duel on a shared board, with a coin flip for who starts and a chat between the two
  players. There is also a practice mode against a computer opponent.
- **First-ever solves.** Most shapes in the gallery have never been solved by a human;
  the first solver of a shape holds that on record.

## Creating your own puzzles
The **Create** page starts from a single sphere; tap to grow any shape in the lattice
(piece counts must be multiples of four). Saved puzzles join the gallery for anyone to
attempt — and creators can record a clip of their shape **building itself sphere by
sphere** to show it off.

## Sharing
Solves and creations can be shared as short vertical **video clips** made for phones —
showing the build, your name, your rank on that puzzle, and an optional personal message —
or as **challenge links** that carry the same message. On phones the clip goes straight to
the sharing menu (TikTok, Instagram, messages) with a ready caption.

## Ask Anton inside the app
This very Q&A is embedded in the app's menus, so players can ask about the puzzle's
mathematics, the Verhoeff story, and the sculpture without leaving the game.

## Practical notes
Free, no ads. Playing needs no account; **saving a solve** (for ranks, challenges, and
leaderboards) uses a simple email sign-in. The app installs to a phone's home screen for a
full-screen experience. Privacy details live at koospuzzle.com/privacy — analytics are
minimal and nothing is sold.
