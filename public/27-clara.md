# Clara — the full story

The full record of Anton's Clara, from his own account; it applies to both the 1 m and the 50 cm
Clara. Visitors who come from a Clara page have usually heard its short and deeper narrations;
this goes further, so answer anything they ask about the piece from here.

## The objects
- **Carrara marble.** Two versions: **1 m tall**, **fixed on a square stepped marble base**; and **50 cm tall on a
  round rotating base** that invites visitors to turn it and explore all sides.
- **One continuous closed ribbon**: it spirals in on one plane and continues into a second spiral on
  another plane. The planes intersect, which is where one spiral becomes the other.
- The **second spiral is the same motif as the first, mirrored**.
- Based on **two spirals on two intersecting planes in the FCC lattice**. The path connects lattice
  vertices; Anton then **interpolates a smooth curve** through the path's points to make it curved.
- (Anton's Wix page calls Clara "a spiraling trefoil knot … from one side it may look like a single
  spiral, from another like two" — the same form, described from the outside.)

## Views and perspective
- Moving around Clara (or turning the small one) makes the **spirals flip upside down**. One flip view:
  face one spiral flat on, with Clara standing on the edge (the blade) of the other spiral; turn, and
  that blade appears on top. The narration states no angles.
- An in-between view looks like a **treble clef**.
- As with all of Anton's work, two people seeing it from different sides see different things, and
  both are right.

## The name
- Anton's partner **Rebecca** plays mostly classical piano on a **Steinway she named Clara, after Clara
  Schumann**. She allows nothing on the piano, not even Anton's coffee, yet she placed his **bronze
  maquette** of this sculpture on it. The sculpture is named after Clara Schumann too.

## Music for the eyes
- Music uses a system of **12 notes**; Anton's work uses **12 vectors**, the directions of the FCC
  lattice.
- He composes with **motifs and symmetry operations**: repeat, reflect, reverse, mirror.
- **Composing sets the constraints; the search finds the shape** within those constraints. Clara was
  found as a unique form in a space of possibilities larger than the number of atoms in the visible
  universe, as all his sculptures are.
- The lattices reflect how atoms bond and organise, the basic structures that make up all matter.

## Meaning
- Spirals stand for **growth**, and also for **spiralling out of control**.
- **"Symmetry is the architecture of beauty"**: the beauty of symmetry should speak for itself.
