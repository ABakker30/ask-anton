# The Portal series (after Tony Smith)

The American sculptor Tony Smith, with his stark, modular geometry in black steel, has long interested
Anton. After an evening spent with Smith's work, Anton made a small collection of his own designs in a
kindred spirit: the Portal series.

Where most of Anton's sculptures are open lattice paths, the Portal pieces are built from flat angular
slabs, each folding around a single clean opening, a portal. They are quieter and more minimal than his
curved or knotted work, closer to architecture than to drawing, and were developed in bronze with a black
patina. The series explores several configurations: two interlocking squares, two rhombic slabs, an
hourglass, a triangle.

One design was realized as a sculpture: Portal, two interlocking rhombic slabs framing a diamond opening,
cast in bronze and finished in black patina, about thirty inches tall.
