# The Portal series (after Tony Smith)

The American sculptor Tony Smith, with his stark, modular geometry in black steel, has long interested
Anton. After an evening spent with Smith's work, Anton made a small collection of his own designs in a
kindred spirit: the Portal series.

Anton's paths are always closed, never open, and the Portal pieces are closed lattice paths too, here
given a flat, slab-like profile so that each folds around a single clean opening, a portal. They are
quieter and more minimal than his curved or knotted work, closer to architecture than to drawing, and were
developed in bronze with a black patina. The series explores several configurations: two interlocking
squares, two rhombic slabs, an hourglass, a triangle.

One design was realized as a sculpture: Portal, two interlocking rhombic slabs framing a diamond opening,
cast in bronze and finished in black patina, about thirty inches tall.
